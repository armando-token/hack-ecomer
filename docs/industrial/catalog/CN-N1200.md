## 2. Technical Summary & Functional Role

The **NOVUS N1200** is an industry-standard 1/16 DIN universal process PID controller engineered for precise temperature control in industrial ovens, autoclaves, furnaces, and heating chambers. It features a multi-sensor universal input supporting Pt100 RTDs, multiple thermocouple types, and linear mA/V process signals with a 15-bit internal analog-to-digital converter (32,767 levels) sampled at 55 samples per second.

In the **Industrial Heating Chamber** pilot process architecture, the N1200 fulfills the **Primary Process PID Controller** role:
- Directly connects to the process temperature sensor (such as a 3-wire Pt100 RTD or thermocouple probe).
- Executes closed-loop PID control using advanced self-tuning and auto-adaptive algorithms to maintain chamber temperature at the setpoint with zero steady-state error.
- Drives an external Solid State Relay (SSR) via its dedicated logic voltage pulse output on terminal `I/O5` (or low-power relays on `I/O1` / `I/O2`).
- Retransmits process variable (PV) or setpoint (SP) as an isolated 4–20 mA current loop.
- Supports Modbus RTU communication over RS-485 for setpoint synchronization with the supervisory HMI (`CN-X5PRIME-HE-XP5`).

### 5.2 Detailed Pinout & Wiring Specifications

#### Power Terminals (`port_pwr_in`)
- **Terminal 1:** Neutral / DC negative power feed.
- **Terminal 2:** Line / DC positive power feed (100–240 Vac/dc ±10%).

#### Universal Process Input (`port_sensor_in`)
Multi-sensor input configurable via parameter `TYPE`:
- **Pt100 RTD (3-wire):**
  - Terminal 11: Current source excitation return / compensation wire.
  - Terminal 12: Positive signal terminal (`+`).
  - Terminal 13: Negative signal terminal (`-`).
  - 3-wire length compensation; excitation current 0.170 mA (α = 0.00385).
  - *2-wire Pt100 note:* Jumper terminals 11 and 12 together.
  - *4-wire Pt100 note:* Leave 4th wire disconnected at controller.
- **Thermocouple (Types J, K, T, E, N, R, S, B):**
  - Terminal 12: Positive leg (`+`).
  - Terminal 13: Negative leg (`-`).
  - Input impedance: > 10 MΩ. Internal cold junction compensation.
- **Current Signals (4–20 mA, 0–20 mA):**
  - Terminal 12: Current input positive (`+`).
  - Terminal 13: Current input negative (`-`).
  - Input impedance: 15 Ω (+2.0 VDC drop @ 20 mA).
- **Voltage Signals (0–50 mV, 0–5 V, 0–10 V):**
  - Terminal 12: Voltage input positive (`+`).
  - Terminal 13: Voltage input negative (`-`).
  - Input impedance: >10 MΩ for 0–50 mV; >1 MΩ for 0–5 V and 0–10 V.

#### Control Relays (`port_io1_relay` & `port_io2_relay`)
- **Terminals 3 & 4 (I/O1):** Normally Open (SPST-NO) dry contact, rated 1.5 A @ 240 Vac.
- **Terminals 5 & 6 (I/O2):** Normally Open (SPST-NO) dry contact, rated 1.5 A @ 240 Vac.

#### Channel I/O5 Multi-Function Output (`port_io5`)
- **Terminals 7 & 8:**
  - *Analog Control / Retransmission Output:* 4–20 mA or 0–20 mA current loop, maximum loop resistance 550 Ω, 31,000 internal resolution levels, galvanically isolated.
  - *SSR Voltage Pulse:* 0/10 V logic pulse output, 20 mA maximum drive capability for direct connection to industrial Solid State Relay control terminals.
  - *Digital Input:* Contact closure / dry contact input for Remote SP, Manual/Auto, or Program Run/Hold.

#### Remote Setpoint Input (`port_remote_sp`)
- **Terminals 9 & 10:** Accepts remote analog setpoint from supervisory system. For 4–20 mA, connect external 100 Ω precision shunt resistor across terminals 9 and 10.

#### RS-485 Communication Port (`port_rs485`)
- **Terminal 16:** `D1 / A` (Non-inverting bidirectional data line).
- **Terminal 17:** `D0 / B` (Inverting bidirectional data line).
- **Terminal 18:** `Common / Shield` (Optional signal reference ground).
- Optical isolation: Yes. Baud rates: 1200 to 115200 bps. 8 data bits, 1 stop bit, parity None/Even/Odd.

## 7. Explicit Missing Capabilities & Non-Documented Aspects

Per MEGAPLAN §9.3, §10.1, §13.4 and the Controlnautas determinism rules:

1. **Relays CANNOT Direct-Drive Industrial Heating Elements:**
   - Internal SPST relays (I/O1, I/O2) are rated for **1.5 A @ 240 Vac resistive**.
   - An industrial chamber heater rated at 2,000 W @ 230 VAC draws approximately 8.7 A. Connecting this heater directly to terminals 3–4 or 5–6 will instantly weld or destroy the internal relay contacts.
   - Direct connection to a heater is flagged as `does_not_meet` (`INSUFFICIENT_CURRENT_RATING`).
   - An intermediate power actuator (Solid State Relay driven by the 10 V logic pulse on `I/O5` or an AC contactor driven by the relay) is required.

2. **SINGLE Process Input Channel (Not Multi-Channel):**
   - Terminals 11–13 provide a single universal input channel. The N1200 **cannot simultaneously measure two physical sensors** (e.g. it cannot measure chamber temperature and chamber humidity concurrently).

3. **CANNOT Interface Directly with RS-485 Sensors on Input:**
   - The universal sensor input requires analog electrical signals (resistance, mV, V, or mA).
   - It **cannot poll digital sensors** like the `CN-THT02` (which communicates via RS-485 Modbus RTU).

4. **NO Built-in Ethernet or CAN Interfaces:**
   - The device does NOT provide RJ45 Ethernet, Modbus TCP, or CAN interfaces. Supervisory networking relies strictly on the 2-wire RS-485 serial bus or USB Mini-B configuration port.

5. **Hardware Option Build Dependencies:**
   - Features such as RS-485 communication, SPDT Relay on I/O3, and 24 VDC power supply are hardware order options. The standard basic model requires 100–240 Vac/dc and uses USB serial for local configuration.
