## 6. Missing Circuit Roles Declaration (Zero Hallucinated SKUs)

In strict accordance with MEGAPLAN §1.3 and §28.3, the incomplete physical loop for the **Industrial Heating Chamber** is formally cataloged:
1. **Direct Process Temperature Sensor for N1200:** N1200 requires a 3-wire Pt100 RTD or thermocouple. THT-02 outputs RS-485 Modbus RTU and cannot connect to N1200 analog terminals. Declared as `MISSING_ROLE: process_temperature_sensor`.
2. **Power Switching Actuator (Solid State Relay):** N1200 OUT1 provides a 5V/50mA logic pulse. Direct heater connection would burn out the controller. Declared as `MISSING_ROLE: power_actuator_ssr`.
3. **Electric Heating Resistance Element:** Sized for chamber volume and heat loss. Declared as `MISSING_ROLE: electric_heater_element`.
4. **24 VDC Instrument Power Supply (PSU):** Required to energize Horner X5 (10-30 VDC) and TZone THT-02 (5-24 VDC) from plant AC mains. Declared as `MISSING_ROLE: instrument_power_supply_24vdc`.
5. **High-Limit Safety Cutoff Thermostat:** Electromechanical thermal protection cutoff. Declared as `MISSING_ROLE: high_limit_safety_thermostat`.

**Zero Fake SKUs:** The upcoming Gate G5 evaluator will evaluate these dependencies deterministically to `not_documented`, never synthesizing artificial catalog SKUs.
