## 1. Objective and Mandate

Gate G6 transitions the Controlnautas platform from the synthetic placeholder asset (`SYN-IND-CTRL-01.glb` in Gate G2) to authoritative, production-grade 3D digital assets for all three verified Gate G4 pilot products in the **Industrial Heating Chamber** (`cámara de calentamiento`) process family:
1. **`CN-X5PRIME-HE-XP5`** (Horner Automation X5 Prime OCS): Supervisory PLC & 4.3" touchscreen operator interface.
2. **`CN-N1200`** (NOVUS Automation N1200 Universal Process Controller): 1/16 DIN precision PID process temperature controller.
3. **`CN-THT02`** (TZone Digital THT-02 Environmental Transmitter): Modbus RTU ambient temperature and relative humidity transmitter probe.

### Key Mandates & Architectural Compliance:
1. **Exact Metric Standardization:** All models are constructed natively in meters (`linear_unit: "m"`), eliminating browser runtime scale guessing.
2. **Standardized Coordinate Frame:** Right-handed Cartesian system (+X Right, +Y Up, +Z Forward). For panel-mount instruments, `Z = 0.000` is the front cutout mounting plane; the front display faces +Z toward the operator, and the rear enclosure extends into -Z.
3. **Pure glTF 2.0 Binary Architecture:** 100% self-contained GLB files with zero required extensions (no Draco, no Meshopt, no KTX2), guaranteeing immediate, universal compatibility with Meta Muse, Three.js, Babylon.js, and WebGPU viewports.
4. **Honest Fidelity Classification (`dimensional_proxy_verified`):** Derived mathematically from manufacturer engineering drawings in official datasheets. No official manufacturer CAD is falsely claimed, and models are not degraded to `illustrative` because external envelopes and terminal locations match physical specifications with **0.0 mm delta**.
5. **PortSchema Topological Binding:** Every electrical port in the Gate G4 technical snapshots (`p_power_in`, `p_rs485`, etc.) maps to a discrete named transform node in the 3D scene hierarchy.
6. **Immutable Content-Addressed Storage (CAS):** Published to SHA-256 storage paths with permanent edge caching (`Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000, immutable`), ETag validation, byte Range requests, and wildcard CORS without credentials.
7. **Database Isolation & Reversibility:** Seeded into PostgreSQL tables `industrial_asset` and `industrial_asset_binding` with zero mutation of core Medusa commerce tables, accompanied by clean rollback scripts.
